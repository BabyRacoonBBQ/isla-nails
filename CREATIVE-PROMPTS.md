# ISLA Tidal Collection — campaign prompts

These prompts are intentionally original. They borrow the visual grammar of luxury jewelry advertising—singular focus, controlled material light, restrained color, and slow motion—without copying a specific campaign or motif.

## Still image prompt

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: full-bleed luxury ecommerce landing-page hero, 16:9 landscape

Create an original couture nail-art campaign image with the visual authority of a high-jewelry advertisement. Show one elegant hand as a sculptural hero object, with all five nails clearly visible. The manicure is island-inspired: translucent lagoon-aqua gel, hand-cut mother-of-pearl shell inlay, one restrained coral-pink accent nail, microscopic champagne-gold edging, sculpted tidal ridges, and a few water droplets. Every layer, cut edge, reflection, brush line, pearl surface, and glossy highlight must be sharply resolved and physically believable.

Backdrop: one seamless, nearly uniform lagoon-turquoise color field, edge-to-edge, with no horizon, room corner, table, beach, or frame. Add exactly two subtle organic accents: one small translucent ivory tropical butterfly and one smaller pale-coral butterfly. They are airy and secondary, never competing with the manicure.

Composition: wide 16:9 cinematic crop. The hand fills roughly 55–65% of the canvas and remains the only dominant subject. Keep calm negative space around the silhouette so a very thin navigation bar and a short line of editorial copy can sit over the image. Natural anatomy, elegant relaxed fingers, all five nails legible, no awkward wrist crop.

Lighting: large diffused studio key, extremely fine rim light tracing the nail silhouette, soft bounce fill, controlled caustic reflections inspired by sunlight under shallow tropical water. Highlights should travel across the curved gel surfaces and reveal their depth. Soft grounded shadow; luminous, serene, rarefied mood.

Color palette: lagoon turquoise #73C9C2, sea-glass mint, mother-of-pearl ivory, restrained coral blush #E78378, champagne gold. Keep saturation elegant, not neon.

Camera and finish: 100 mm macro lens look, high dynamic range, deep enough focus to keep every nail crisp, premium beauty retouching while retaining real skin texture, 4K product-ad sharpness, photorealistic.

No text, no logo, no jewelry, no four-leaf-clover motif, no recognizable brand design, no watermark. Avoid generic nail-salon stock photography, busy tropical scenery, sand, palms, shell props, excessive glitter, extra hands, extra fingers, warped anatomy, plastic skin, overlong impractical nails, heavy vignette, borders, and UI elements.
```

## Video prompt (8-second seamless hero loop)

```text
Create an 8-second, 16:9, 4K, 24 fps seamless luxury beauty campaign loop for a full-screen ecommerce homepage.

Scene: a single elegant hand wearing an original island-inspired couture manicure floats against a completely seamless lagoon-turquoise studio color field. The five nails are always readable: translucent aqua gel with visible internal depth, mother-of-pearl shell inlays, one soft coral accent, microscopic champagne-gold edges, sculpted wave relief, and a few water droplets. Two tiny butterflies—one translucent ivory, one pale coral—move gently at the outer edges as subtle visual punctuation.

Motion choreography:
0–2 seconds: begin in an extreme macro three-quarter view. The hand is almost still while a thin ribbon of light travels across the curved nail surfaces, revealing gel depth, shell iridescence, gold edges, and precise craftsmanship.
2–6 seconds: the hand rotates only 12–15 degrees in one slow, continuous, physically natural movement, as if suspended in calm shallow water. The camera performs an almost imperceptible 3% push-in. One butterfly crosses a short arc in the far background; the second only opens its wings once. Caustic light moves more slowly than the hand.
6–8 seconds: motion eases back toward the opening pose and lighting state so the final frame matches the first frame perfectly for an invisible loop.

Camera: stabilized 100 mm macro-lens look, shallow but controlled depth of field; every nail remains sharp and the background stays soft and uninterrupted. No cuts, no speed ramps, no camera shake, no focus hunting.

Lighting and material behavior: diffused studio key, precise rim light, soft bounce fill, realistic refraction through translucent gel, restrained iridescence in shell, crisp but never blown highlights, physically accurate reflections, real skin microtexture. The quality must feel like high-jewelry macro cinematography, not a salon reel.

Composition: the hand occupies 55–65% of frame, centered slightly right, with protected negative space at the top and lower-left for transparent website navigation and short copy. Nothing may enter or leave frame abruptly. The background must remain a nearly uniform #73C9C2 color block at every moment.

No text, logo, jewelry, clover motif, recognizable brand signature, watermark, beach, sand, palm trees, shell props, glitter storm, extra hand, extra finger, anatomy deformation, finger morphing, nail-shape drift, object popping, flicker, temporal texture crawl, changing nail design, changing skin tone, abrupt butterfly motion, hard vignette, border, or UI.
```

## Export notes

- Desktop master: 3840×2160, H.265 or ProRes master; web delivery as muted autoplay MP4 plus WebM.
- Mobile alternate crop: 1080×1920 with the hand slightly higher and protected space around the centered logo.
- Keep the first frame as the poster image so video loading does not cause a visual jump.
- Aim for a 6–12 MB desktop web file and a 3–6 MB mobile file; strip audio entirely.
