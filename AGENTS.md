# Project rules

## Original assets

- `raw_data/` is the source-of-truth asset archive.
- Never modify, rename, move, delete, compress, crop, or overwrite anything under `raw_data/`.
- Website-ready derivatives must be written outside `raw_data/` (for example, `public/images/202609/`).
- When adding or updating product imagery, read from `raw_data/` and preserve the original files unchanged.
